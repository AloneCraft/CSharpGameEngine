# Changelog

- SDK 0.1.35: L4 IStageRenderBackend/IStageShaderArtifactへ実提出・フレーム再生・成果物閉包を接続。FrameStateがNativeクラス/光源kindを直接参照する依存を除いた。クリア値/オブジェクト余力/Debugホットリロード能力も共通値を正本にする。ServerのNative作成口とパス計画は引き続き移行中。
- SDK 0.1.34: L4 RenderViewSourceが値として透視/明示VP/固定Viewの再生成入力を保持。FrameStateからL5 RenderCameraと旧行列保存分岐を除去し、リサイズ/復旧時の自動aspect・明示aspect・固定行列の契約を維持。L5 Policyが提出形式を選び、解決時の投影検証と計算はL4で共有する。
- SDK 0.1.33: Stage/RenderServerの公開診断をRendering.Backend.RenderDeviceMessageへ変更。Native例外はRenderBackendFailureExceptionを継承し、HRESULTから回復理由への分類をNative側へ移動。サーバーは分類済み理由を扱い、未知の失敗は復旧要求にしない。
- SDK 0.1.32: シーン用Camera/CameraProjectionとPrimitiveMesh/PrimitiveTypeをL5 Runtime.Windowsの共有ソースへ移動。完全修飾名とシーンJSONの語彙を維持。プリミティブ組立はPrimitiveRoster.Build、共通の接線生成はL4 StageMeshGeometryへ移し、RenderServerのプリミティブ語彙依存を除去した。
- SDK 0.1.31: CPU読み戻し値 `PixelColor` / `PixelReadbackResult` をポータブルなRenderingへ移動。公開FormatをDXGI_FORMATから `RenderPixelFormat` へ変更し、Native側で形式を対応付ける。画素バイト列・行間隔・BGRA復元を維持し、生成エディターブリッジも新しい型を返す。
- SDK 0.1.30: L4の検証済み `RenderView` とL5 `RenderViewPolicy`、Native/RenderServerのSetViewを追加。投影/深度/補助投影/位置/露出/カメラ基底を値として提出・復旧できる。旧可視性クエリ値RenderViewはRenderVisibilityViewへ改名。旧カメラ/行列APIのリサイズ復旧メタデータは後続のアダプター分離まで維持する。
- SDK 0.1.29: SetCamera前に描く既定Stageも対数深度で初期化するよう修正。透視行列だけを設定し線形の深度パケットが残る起動時の抜けを、通常のカメラ初期化処理を共有して解消した。
- SDK 0.1.28: StageのFrame/Object定数配置をL4の型付き `StageGpuLayouts` に移し、D3D12側の35個の手書きオフセットを削除。L7がDXCの実反射データを取得し、行列の行配置を含めてStage/Lumenと照合する。FrameCB960/ ObjectCB192バイトとstage-v14を維持する。
- SDK 0.1.27: 描画カメラ数学を `Graphics.D3D12.Camera` からポータブルな `Rendering.View.RenderCamera` へ移動。シーンComponentのCameraとは別の公開型。計算・既定値を維持し、既存のカメラ数学テストもRendering.Testsへ移した。カメラ提出方針とフレーム状態の分離はM0120で継続する。
- SDK 0.1.26: 環境・メッシュ・材質・発光パッチの提出値を `CSharpGameEngine.Rendering`、物理光量・影予算・カスケード・発光パッチ生成のCPU計算を同 `.Pipeline` へ移動。D3D12提出アダプターを分離した。計算式・シェーダーと画素基準は維持する。M0120の層分離は継続中。

- Added Lumen-like dynamic global illumination and reflections (ADR-0078/M0111). A surface cache of six box cards per object (captured in the mesh's own space, lit every frame within a budget by the sun, local lights, the previous frame's radiance cache, the sky and emission including `MeshEmissive`), a screen probe final gather (screen traces, then the global distance field confirmed on the mesh distance fields, then a world-anchored clipmap radiance cache) and GGX reflections (screen, distance field, sky; rough lobes read the probes). Unreal's scalability decides it: `RenderScalability` High or Epic runs Lumen, Medium and below keep the DDGI probe volume of ADR-0069 (bit-identical to before). The default is High on a hardware GPU and Medium on WARP; `CGE_SCALABILITY_GI`/`CGE_SCALABILITY_REFLECTIONS` override it and `CGE_LUMEN_VISUALIZE` shows the diffuse, reflections or surface cache. `PostProcessVolume` gains Unreal's Lumen settings (defaults are Unreal's, read with `tools/UnrealExport/probe_lumen.py`). The Stage shader ABI is now `stage-v13` (`LumenParams`, t16/t17, `LumenSurfacePSMain`, `LumenCardPSMain`). SDK 0.1.23. Not verified on a hardware GPU; Epic on WARP intermittently lost the device in long test runs (unresolved).
- Fixed the Vehicle template's wheels and the car's crashes (ADR-0078/M0109 follow-up). The scene's wheel rest rotations were 0 and a half turn, so the imported wheel meshes (axle along F) stood with their treads sideways; they are now -0.25 turns on the left and +0.25 on the right, as the body and glass already were and as UE's wheel components are (yaw -90 left, +90 right; the X mirror of the left wheels is not implemented). A car hitting a wall at 100 km/h used to spin (yaw -2.7 rad/s, roll -5.5 rad/s) and fall on its side: a tilted box face meets a wall with its lower corners a few millimetres inside and the upper ones millimetres short (30 m/s is half a metre a tick), so the contact patch held two points and took the whole impact. Corners of a box within 5 cm above a mesh face now join the patch as speculative points (negative penetration; held like touching points when they reach the face in that step, free otherwise); the bounce is taken once per point from the approach speed before the solve, and the impulse of an impact step is no longer carried into the next step as a support force (it threw the stopped body back off the surface). The gear shows N during a shift as UE does (0.2 s through neutral; about 85 km/h for 1st to 2nd), which is unchanged. `CGE_AUTOMATION_CAMERA=r,u,f,yaw,pitch` films a run from a camera at a world offset from the Entity the scene camera hangs from. SDK is now 0.1.22. The Vehicle WARP drive travels 57.150 m (was 52.014 m; the wall no longer throws the car back) with the same 30.5 degree turn; ThirdPerson, FirstPerson and TopDown are unchanged.
